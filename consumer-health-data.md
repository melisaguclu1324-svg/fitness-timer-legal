# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

This policy supplements our [Privacy Policy](privacy-en.html). It applies to residents of Washington State and Nevada, and to anyone whose consumer health data is collected there, under the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law.

**Who we are:** Melisa Güçlü, publisher of the Fonksiyonel Fitness Timer mobile app (the "App").
**Contact:** melisaguclu@protonmail.com

## 1. Consumer health data we collect

"Consumer health data" is personal information that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. In the App this is limited to fitness information you choose to record while signed in:

- completed workouts: mode, duration, rounds, work and rest time, date;
- scores you save: workout name, time, rounds and reps, weight lifted;
- what is derived from them: personal records, XP, streak, badges and league.

We do **not** collect the following:

- **Heart rate.** Heart rate read from a Bluetooth strap is shown on your screen only. It is not stored and never leaves your device.
- **Health Connect data.** With your permission the App writes a finished workout and estimated calories to Health Connect on your device. It never reads from Health Connect, and this data is not sent to our servers.
- Precise location, biometric or genetic data, or information about medical conditions, treatment or reproductive health.

If you use the timers without an account, your workouts stay on your device and we collect no consumer health data.

## 2. Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: saving your workout history, syncing it across your devices, calculating personal records, XP, streaks, badges and leaderboards. We do not use consumer health data for advertising, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond this without asking for your consent first.

## 3. Where it comes from

Directly from you, when you complete a workout or save a score while signed in.

## 4. How we share it

We do **not** sell consumer health data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

- **Processors.** Supabase Inc. stores it for us (database servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and may only process it on our instructions.
- **Other users of the App.** Your username and your best score for a workout appear on that workout's leaderboard. If community features are enabled and you choose to share a workout, other users can see it.
- **Authorities,** where the law requires it.

We have no affiliates.

Our advertising provider (Google AdMob) receives your advertising ID, IP address and device information, but not your workout data.

## 5. Your rights

You can:

- ask whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it;
- ask for the list of third parties we have shared it with, and how to contact them;
- withdraw your consent to collection or sharing;
- have it deleted.

**How to use them:** to delete everything immediately, open the App and go to Profile > Settings > "Delete My Account and All Data". For any other request, email melisaguclu@protonmail.com from the address on your account. We answer within 45 days.

**Appeals:** if we refuse a request, reply to our decision within 45 days and say that you are appealing. We answer appeals in writing within 45 days. If the appeal is refused, Washington residents can contact the Washington State Attorney General at <https://www.atg.wa.gov/file-complaint>, and Nevada residents can contact the Nevada Attorney General.

We do not treat you differently for using these rights.

## 6. Changes

If we change what we collect or how we share it, we update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
